Stack-Farm 🌱

    How the real app works
    1. Photo
    A farmer or field agent takes a photo of one leaf on a basic phone.
    2. Clean up
    Image processing crops the leaf, fixes lighting and resizes it.
    3. Detect
    A TensorFlow CNN trained on leaf images names the disease and how sure it is.
    4. Act
    A simple Streamlit screen shows three plain steps the farmer can take today.
